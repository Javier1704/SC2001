# SC2001: Algorithm Design and Analysis

## Lab Projects Overview & Guidelines

### 1. Schedule & Team Allocation
1. Lab sessions start in **Week 6 or 7**, depending on your lab group.
2. Students in each lab group will be allocated into teams of **3 – 4 members**. 
   - Team allocation will be done by the lab TA and released on the lab group site in NTULearn.
   - **Do not form your own teams!**
3. Team members should work together to complete the projects. In general, all members of the same team will receive the same grade for each project.
   - The lab TA may award a higher grade to a student with outstanding performance.
   - The lab TA may award a lower grade to a student with very little contribution (e.g., lack of active participation).

---

### 2. Attendance & Policies
4. **Absence**: If a student is absent from a lab session, submit your MC/LOA to the lab staff and your Lab TA. Teammates must present on behalf of the absentee.
5. **Compulsory Attendance**: It is compulsory to attend project presentations.
   - Absence without valid justification (e.g., MC) results in **zero marks** for that project.
   - If on MC for both Lab 1 and Lab 2, an appointment must be made with the Lab TA to answer questions regarding both labs to obtain the marks.

---

### 3. Preparation & Presentation
6. **Preparation**: Before each lab class, each team must complete the practical work (coding and testing).
   - Any programming language may be used (e.g., Python, Java, C++, C).
   - Code must be clean and well-documented so that someone unfamiliar with the chosen language can follow it.
7. **Presentation Format**:
   - **10 minutes total** per team: presentation of algorithms, demonstrations, and 2 minutes of Q&A.
   - Strictly timed: no additional time will be given.
   - Every team member must be familiar with the entire project; the Lab TA may select any member to answer questions during Q&A (assessed under presentation).
8. **Submission**:
   - **No submission** is required for Lab Projects 1 & 2.
   - Presentation slides for **Project 3** must be submitted to your lab group course site by the end of the day of Quiz 2.

---

### 4. Assessment Rubric

Each project is assessed based on the following components:

| Component | Description | Weightage |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Correctness** | Algorithm correctness and implementation quality | **40%** |
| **Analysis** | Theoretical and empirical analysis | **40%** |
| **Presentation** | Clarity of slides, demonstration, and Q&A | **20%** |
| **Total** | | **100%** |
